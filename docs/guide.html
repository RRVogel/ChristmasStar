Your Christmas Star

Setting up — a simple guide

What’s in the box

The star11 lights. It sits on the very top of the tree.
The light string49 lights, wrapped around the control box.
The control boxWith a knob you can turn and push.

Power: it runs from a USB phone charger plug, using the USB-C cable. Please use a charger that says 2 A (2 amps) or more on it — most modern phone or tablet chargers are fine.

Putting it on the tree

  1. Pop the star on the top of the tree.
  2. The light string comes wrapped around the control box. Starting just under the star, unwrap it from the box as you go round and round the tree, working downwards.
  3. When the string is all unwrapped, set the control box under the tree, close enough for the USB cable to reach the charger.

Switching on

Plug the cable into the charger and switch it on at the wall. The lights come on with a white star and a colourful tree.

The knob

TURN it
to make the lights brighter or dimmer. Clockwise makes them brighter.
PUSH it
to change to the next light show.

The little light in the knob changes colour to show which light show is on. (There’s a list of them all on the last page.)

Keep pushing to reach Off. Push once more to start again from the beginning.

The music shows

A tiny microphone in the control box listens to music in the room. If the lights hardly react, turn the knob to turn up the sensitivity (in the music shows, the knob sets how sensitive it is instead of brightness), or play the music a bit louder or nearer.

That’s all you need! The knob does everything. The phone part on the next page is optional — it just gives you a handy remote control on your iPhone.

Connecting to your iPhone (first time only)

When the star needs your Wi-Fi, the knob blinks blue. Then:

  1. On your iPhone, open Settings, then tap Wi-Fi.
  2. Tap Christmas Star Setup. The password is: starlight
  3. A page should pop up by itself. If it doesn’t, open Safari and type 192.168.4.1 in the address bar.
  4. Tap Configure WiFi.
  5. Tap the name of your home Wi-Fi in the list.
  6. Type your home Wi-Fi password, then tap Save.

Your phone goes back to your normal Wi-Fi. The knob flashes green when the star is connected. Done!

Tip: if your iPhone says “no internet connection”, that’s normal here — choose to stay connected (or use without internet).
If the star couldn’t join, the setup page will tell you. Check the Wi-Fi password and try again.

Using your phone as a remote

With your phone on your home Wi-Fi, open Safari and type christmasstar.local

Handy: tap the Share button (the square with an arrow), then Add to Home Screen. Next time, just tap the icon.

On that page you can:

  • Turn the lights off and on again with the power button at the top.
  • Pick any light show.
  • Make the lights brighter or dimmer.
  • Change how much the music shows react to sound, and how fast Color chaser goes.

Updates

Now and then the star quietly checks over the Wi-Fi for little improvements from Rick. You don’t need to do anything. While it updates, the knob glows purple — please don’t unplug it then.

If something goes wrong

  • Unplug it, wait 10 seconds, and plug it back in.
  • If the knob keeps blinking blue, it has lost the Wi-Fi. Just do the iPhone steps at the top of this page again.
Please keep it indoors and away from water.
